Image Guidelines

  • Use a clear, high-resolution fundus photograph
  • Ensure the retina is well-centered in the image
  • Good lighting — avoid glare or dark images
  • File format: JPG or PNG only
  • Maximum file size: 10 MB
  • Do not use blurry or obstructed images
  • Avoid heavily compressed or edited photos
