Understanding the Disease

What is Diabetic Retinopathy?

Diabetic retinopathy (DR) is a diabetes complication that affects the eyes by damaging the blood vessels in the retina. It is a leading cause of blindness worldwide, yet it is largely preventable through early detection and timely treatment.

NetraVision classifies retinopathy into five internationally recognized severity levels, giving you a precise understanding of the condition's progression.

Level 0 — No DR Normal retina, no signs of damage
Level 1 — Mild DR Minor microaneurysms present
Level 2 — Moderate DR More extensive vascular damage
Level 3 — Severe DR Significant retinal ischemia
Level 4 — Proliferative DR New vessel growth, high risk of blindness
