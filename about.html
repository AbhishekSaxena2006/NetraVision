About NetraVision

Bridging the gap between artificial intelligence and accessible healthcare through innovative research and technology.

Who We Are

Bringing AI-Powered Vision to Healthcare

NetraVision is a college research project developed by a passionate team of Computer Science students. Our goal is to demonstrate how Convolutional Neural Networks can be applied to real-world medical challenges — specifically the detection and classification of diabetic retinopathy.

Diabetic retinopathy is a leading cause of preventable blindness. By training a CNN on thousands of labeled retinal fundus images, we have built a system that can classify the disease across five internationally recognized severity levels with high accuracy.

Project Context

A College Research Project

NetraVision was built as part of our Computer Science final-year project. Each team member was responsible for a specific module: dataset preparation, model training, backend API, and frontend development.

We used the APTOS 2019 Blindness Detection dataset from Kaggle, which contains thousands of retinal fundus images labeled by trained clinicians.

Our CNN model was built using TensorFlow and Keras, with a custom architecture fine-tuned from established pre-trained models. The web interface you're using is the frontend — built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
